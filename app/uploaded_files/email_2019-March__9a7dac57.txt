should be collected separately from non-halogenated waste:

and waste.

My two cents'...? This looks fine to me.? My only concern would be 
managing the fluoroether solvent, stripper, and developer materials.?

lifting for others) and they have low vapor pressure, so they won't 
evaporate.? I expect they might be like oil, so any spills, drips, or

the like, but would suggest taking similar precautions. In addition, I 
would suggest that he use his own dedicated labware for developing and 
stripping.
