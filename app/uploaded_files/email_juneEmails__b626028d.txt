Hi Leah,

Our concerns around new materials are less about what you're using it for, and more about the dangers of working with the materials and how to mitigate them.  Do you have any SDS information for this material or the component elements (Especially La and Li, which we are not as familiar with).  Do any of these materials vaporize or volatilize at low temperatures (<800C) or outgas in high vacuum?

Also is your process limited to sputter coating Ti and Pt on to solids of this material?  Will this require elevated temperatures that might vaporize or volatilize any of the component elements?  Are you planning on etching or machining this material in our facility? We're concerned about any steps or processes that start to break down the material leaving any residue of the material or its constitutive elements.

Sorry if these are overly broad, but these are questions we're asking when evaluating how to work safely with a material.

Hi Leah,



Our concerns around new materials are less about what you're using it for, and more about the dangers of working with the materials and how to mitigate them.  Do you have any SDS information for this material or the component elements (Especially La and Li, which we are not as familiar with).  Do any of these materials vaporize or volatilize at low temperatures (<800C) or outgas in high vacuum?



Also is your process limited to sputter coating Ti and Pt on to solids of this material?  Will this require elevated temperatures that might vaporize or volatilize any of the component elements?  Are you planning on etching or machining this material in our facility? We're concerned about any steps or processes that start to break down the material leaving any residue of the material or its constitutive elements.



Sorry if these are overly broad, but these are questions we're asking when evaluating how to work safely with a material.
