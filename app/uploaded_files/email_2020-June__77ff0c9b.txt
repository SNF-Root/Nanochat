Dear Yujo and Wilman,

As far as I know there is no chamber clean process available for CeO2.   If the chamber is contaminated with metal oxides it is mechanically cleaned by external vendor.

We often done depositions of multi-component metal oxide films in the same chamber and don?t see contamination from element to element by XRF when using thermal ALD.

On the other hand, we don?t use the same chamber for deposition of LowK films and metal oxide films.  Traces of metal contamination will impact dielectric quality of SiO2 films deposited in the same chamber.  Thus, other teams may see trace contamination with Ce or Hf if the same chamber is used interchangeably to deposit SiO2 for accurate dielectric measurements.

I don?t see any issues if the chamber is dedicated to deposition of metal oxide films.  You still have to be careful to avoid chemical condensation in the lines and on the chamber walls.   Essentially, avoid any cold spots.  This is important for any chemical with low vapor pressure.

When we switch from metal oxide deposition project (not form wafer to wafer) we deposit 50-100 nm of aluminum oxide from TMA and make sure that TMA process matches baseline.  If we can?t match the baseline, the chamber is disassembled and cleaned.  You may consider to run TMA before your HfO2/CeO2 project to ?passivate? the chamber from prior depositions.

Hopefully this is helpful.

Best regards,
Sergei
