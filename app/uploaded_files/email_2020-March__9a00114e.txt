Hi PROM Comm,

Back in the days, we had 2 previous users who tried to actually grow
Li-containing films in the Savannah, and after their depositions we removed
the chamber for cleaning. We also had a user who tried growing the
Li-containing films in Fiji3, and he coated the chamber with 100 cycles of
Al2O3 after his deposition. We did VPD-ICPMS and verified that the Li
contamination did become dramatically reduced after the chamber coating,
but it was not zero. This may have been due to the fact that he used the
same tweezers to handle the known Li-contaminated wafers as well as the
post chamber coat wafers. This was in Fiji3, and we warned the community
about the contamination potential and users checked in with me after their
runs and they did not detect any impact to their devices (Fiji3 is
primarily used for gate oxide growth, although infrequently).

So- I think that in this case, since his materials are stable and the
contamination risk should be much lower than using Li precursors, doing a
100 cycle post chamber coat should be sufficient. We can also ask for
VPD-ICPMS verification if we would like, since we are definitely
extrapolating quite a bit here.

What do you think?

Michelle
