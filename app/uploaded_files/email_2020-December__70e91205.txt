> Mahnaz,
>
> Lanthanum and cobalt do not have any volatile by-products in PT-MTL
> chemistries and so, I would recommend using Ion Mill for this application
> and not PT-MTL.  I am not sure if Stephanie has asked for PROM approval to
> etch these materials in PT-MTL.  Another factor to consider is how to
> attach the sample to the carrier wafer.  Kapton tape, though it may secure
> the substrate, is not a good option as it does not provide heat transfer
> during etch (including Ion Mill process) and the potential for resist
> burning is very high.  We should really discuss these before you procced
> any further.  Let us fix a time to talk about your process requirements.

> Usha
