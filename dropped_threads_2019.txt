but it's up to you as to how to make your project.? Rather like learning
how to use the tools in a machine shop to make your own custom
motorcycle.? We can show you how to make the parts, but not how to put
them together.

We're a lab for tinkerers.? Now, I know your next question:? can you
hook me up with some of your tinkerers?? Well, we do have a list of
independent consultants -- they are not affiliated with Stanford, but
are qualified labmembers -- and you are welcome to contact them (there's
a list on our website.)? However, I wouldn't recommend this unless there
is expertise on OLED fabrication in your startup.? Like the motorcycle,
the consultants can make your parts but no guarantee they will all fit
together into the ride you envisioned.

I don't mean to be discouraging, but rather, set some expectations
before we get too far into the process.? I'd be glad to chat if you'd
like to discuss further.
